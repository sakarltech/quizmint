Real materials, fine brushed metal grain visible on the bevel, subtle enamel imperfections, physically credible soft highlights, realistic restrained reflection and self-shadowing. It should look like a premium tangible emblem photographed with a macro product lens, not a cartoon, not inflated plastic, not a stylized 3D illustration. Elegant, simple, memorable, professionally finished educational technology brand, clear silhouette at small sizes. Single isolated emblem centered and filling about 82% of the square canvas on a genuinely transparent background. No words, no typography, no watermark, no hands, no props, no stars, no extra checkmark, no pedestal, no ground plane, no external cast shadow. Keep the central hole transparent.
